💡 Grammar Clue

than → use comparative

the → use superlative

bigger / faster / cuter / scarier

biggest / fastest / cutest / scariest

Look for than

A seahorse is cuter than a dolphin.
Sharks are faster than whales.

Look for the

A baby seal is the cutest.
Dolphins are the fastest.
